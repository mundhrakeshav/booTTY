A · Slot dot

Conservative
Size
6 pt solid dot.
Where
The tab-colour slot before the title. Collapsed: a badge on the number. Native: beside ⌘N.
Tells apart
Hue only.
Motion
160 ms fade in, then still.
Tab colour
Hidden by the status until it clears.
Risk
Same shape as a tab colour, so a green tab reads as finished. Weak under red–green colour blindness.
Build
ColorDot and TabColorIndicatorView take a status override.

B · Ringed dot

Recommended
Size
5 pt core, 1 pt gap, 1 pt ring: 9 pt overall.
Where
The tab-colour slot. Collapsed: left of the number. Native: beside ⌘N.
Tells apart
Motion and type: waiting pulses; done bolds the title until you view it.
Motion
Done pings once (0.9 s). Waiting pulses every 2.4 s until answered. Reduced motion: both still, and the waiting ring thickens to 1.5 pt.
Tab colour
Kept as the fill; the ring carries status. Rings only ever mean status.
Risk
A question left unanswered keeps pulsing.
Build
New StatusDot (circle, ring, repeating pulse) in the ColorDot slot; title weight follows status. Native: accessory view plus NSWindowTab.attributedTitle.

C · Trailing shapes

Divergent
Size
6 pt dot; 5.5 pt diamond, 7.8 pt tip to tip.
Where
Replaces ⌘N at the end of the row. Collapsed: right of the number. Native: replaces the ⌘N label.
Tells apart
Shape: circle or diamond, readable in grayscale.
Motion
180 ms pop in, then still.
Tab colour
Untouched: identity leads, status trails.
Risk
Off the scan line of a left-docked bar; hovering swaps it for ×. In native tabs it lands beside the tab-colour dot.
Build
The trailing ZStack in VerticalTabRow swaps the shortcut Text; native swaps keyEquivalentLabel.

Notes

  • booTTY has to draw these. A program can only set a tab's title text, and the only coloured characters text can carry are emoji, which have a fixed size and colour. The omp extension would send a status instead, and the tab bar draws the dot.
  • Shared rules. Green clears when you view the tab; amber clears when you answer. A finish in the tab you're viewing gets no dot, since the whistle covers it. Working has no dot: omp's spinner in the title already shows it. A tab with several panes shows its most urgent one.
